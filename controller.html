Custom Arcade Game Controller

Made from a PowerA controller for Nintendo Switch.

The controller is dismantled and arcade buttons are soldered on to contact pads on the circuit board.

The casing is laser cut from 3mm acrylic and the LED lights are powered by 8 AA battries.
