3D Printed Minature Working Escalator

- Modeled based on how real escalators work using reference images.

- Includes parts such as Step Chain, Sprockets, Step Tracks, and Step Chain Tracks

- A 6v DC Motor with gearbox and a H-bridge circuit is used to drive the escalator.
