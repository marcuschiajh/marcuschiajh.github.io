Birefringence of Ice

To build a prototype to show the birefringence of ice. Birefringence means double refraction. Birefringent material bends and split rays when it passes through it. A spectrum of colours can be seen through a polarizer when polarized light is shined through the birefringent material.


The original method to create the birefringence of ice is to pour a bit of water on the dish and put it in the fridge to freeze it and then place it on top of a polarized light source.

This method is not practical as an exhibit as:
- The thin ice will melt eventually
- The process requires manpower to keep on replacing the sample

The solution to make it self sustaining is to use a Thermoelectric Cooler TEC to:
- To freeze the water to form a thin layer of ice
- At the same time shine polarized light through it
