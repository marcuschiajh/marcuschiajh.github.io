BagPal - RFID personal belongings tracking

NTU Group project for course MA2079 - Engineering Innovation and Design


Problem - Many people forget important items such as wallet, keys, laptops, when leaving home or moving between places. Existing solutions do not provide any ways to track or confirm what is inside the bag for the average consumer at an affordable price. As a result users may experience inconvenience and stress over forgotten or misplaced items.

Solution - To make an integrated solution consisting of a smart module and an application that detect and track items that are in a bag. It alerts the user if something is missing, improving daily efficiency.
